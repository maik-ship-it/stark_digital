## Why Google Ads is a natural fit for dental practices

Dental searches are intensely local and often urgent. "Emergency dentist dublin", "dentist open Saturday dublin", "toothache walk-in dublin" — these are people who need an appointment today, not next month.

That urgency means intent is extremely high. Someone searching "dental implants dublin" or "Invisalign cost dublin" is far along in their decision-making process. They're not browsing — they're comparing providers and looking for a reason to book.

Google Ads puts your clinic in front of these patients at exactly the right moment. Done correctly, it's one of the most cost-effective marketing channels available to a Dublin dental practice.

## The two search modes: emergency and elective

Dental Google Ads works across two very different patient types, and your campaigns should reflect this.

### Emergency searches (high urgency, immediate decision)

These patients are in pain and want help today. They're searching:
- "emergency dentist dublin"
- "dentist open now dublin"
- "dental pain relief dublin"
- "cracked tooth dublin"
- "dentist saturday dublin"

For emergency searches, **speed of response is your biggest selling point**. Your ad copy should emphasise availability — "Open 7 Days", "Same-Day Appointments", "Evening Slots Available". Your landing page needs a visible phone number at the top and a frictionless booking form.

### Elective / cosmetic searches (considered purchase)

These patients are researching and comparing. They're searching:
- "Invisalign dublin"
- "teeth whitening dublin price"
- "dental implants cost dublin"
- "veneers dublin"
- "composite bonding dublin"

For cosmetic searches, **social proof and pricing transparency win**. Your ads should highlight before/after results, financing options, and specific treatment names. These patients often need more information before booking, so a lead magnet (free consultation, price guide download) works well.

## Local targeting: getting it right in Dublin

Dublin's dental market is hyper-local. A patient in Rathmines is unlikely to travel to Clontarf for routine care — but they might for specialist cosmetic treatment.

Target your campaigns accordingly:

| Treatment type | Recommended radius |
|---|---|
| General / emergency dentistry | 5–8 km from clinic |
| Orthodontics (Invisalign, braces) | 10–15 km |
| Implants / cosmetic | 15–20 km (patients will travel) |
| Specialist referrals | Dublin-wide |

Use **location extensions** (now called Business Profile extensions) so your address, phone number, and clinic hours appear directly in your ads.

## Essential negative keywords for dental Google Ads

Poor negative keyword lists are the most common reason dental campaigns waste budget. Before launch, exclude:

- Job-related: "dental nurse jobs", "dental receptionist", "dental technician course"
- NHS/UK terms: "NHS dentist", "NHS treatment", "free NHS"
- Educational: "dental school ireland", "dentistry course", "how to become a dentist"
- Irrelevant treatments not offered: (customise based on your services)
- DIY: "fix tooth at home", "temporary filling kit"

A thorough negative keyword list from day one can reduce wasted spend by 20–35%.

## What a well-run dental campaign looks like

1. **Separate ad groups for each treatment type** — don't mix emergency and cosmetic in the same campaign; different bidding strategies apply
2. **Call tracking** — many patients call rather than fill in a form; track calls as conversions
3. **Appointment booking tracking** — if you use online booking software, track completed appointments directly
4. **Ad scheduling** — run emergency ads 24/7; scale back cosmetic ads to business hours when staff can respond
5. **Responsive Search Ads** — test multiple headlines and descriptions; Google's algorithm identifies what converts best
6. **Landing pages that match the search** — an Invisalign ad should go to an Invisalign page, not your homepage

## Budget guidance for Dublin dental clinics

| Monthly Ad Spend | Realistic outcome |
|---|---|
| €500–€800/month | Good for a single-location clinic with limited capacity |
| €800–€1,500/month | Consistent new patient flow; cosmetic + general mix |
| €1,500–€3,000/month | High-volume growth; suitable for multi-chair or group practices |

For cosmetic-focused clinics, higher budgets are easier to justify — a single dental implant case (€2,000–€4,000) justifies significant ad spend.

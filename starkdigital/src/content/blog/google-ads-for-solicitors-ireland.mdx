## Why Google Ads works particularly well for solicitors

Legal services have some of the highest commercial intent on Google. When someone searches for "conveyancing solicitor Dublin" or "employment law solicitor Ireland", they're not browsing — they need help now.

That's the ideal condition for Google Ads.

## The keyword landscape for Irish solicitors

There are broadly three types of keywords to consider:

**High-intent service terms** — "conveyancing solicitor Dublin", "personal injury claim Ireland", "family law solicitor Cork". These are expensive but convert extremely well.

**Problem-based searches** — "landlord not returning deposit Ireland", "what to do after a car accident Ireland". Lower competition, high intent.

**Local terms** — "solicitor near me", "solicitor Dublin 2". Essential for any firm with a physical location.

## What does it actually cost?

For Dublin-based solicitors, typical CPCs (cost per click) in 2025:

- Conveyancing: €8–€20/click
- Personal injury: €12–€30/click
- Family law: €6–€15/click
- Employment law: €5–€12/click
- General queries: €3–€8/click

A realistic starting budget is **€1,000–€2,000/month** in ad spend.

## Compliance considerations

Solicitors advertising in Ireland must be careful about:

- Claims around outcomes ("we win 95% of cases" — avoid)
- Comparative advertising
- Regulated terms and financial promotions (if applicable)

We understand these requirements and build compliant campaigns from the start.

## What a well-run campaign looks like

The best-performing campaigns we run for solicitors share these characteristics:

1. **Tight geographic targeting** — usually 20–30km radius around the office
2. **Exact and phrase match keywords** — not broad match, which wastes budget
3. **Negative keyword lists** — excluding irrelevant searches from day one
4. **Call tracking** — knowing which keywords drove phone enquiries
5. **Landing pages that match the search intent** — not just the homepage

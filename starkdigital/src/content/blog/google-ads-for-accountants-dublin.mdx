## Why Google Ads works differently for accountants

When someone searches "emergency dentist dublin", they want help in the next hour. When someone searches "accountant dublin", they're usually still in research mode — comparing options, checking credentials, asking colleagues.

That difference matters enormously for how you run Google Ads.

Accounting is a high-trust, relationship-driven service. The decision cycle is longer. But that's not a reason to avoid paid search — it's a reason to structure your campaigns around it.

<Callout type="stat" title="The opportunity">
  **68% of accountancy searches in Ireland happen on Google.** Most firms rely on referrals alone, leaving significant search traffic uncaptured by their competitors.
</Callout>

## The keyword landscape for Dublin accountants

The most valuable searches fall into four categories:

**Service-specific terms** — "small business accountant dublin", "company accounts ireland", "VAT returns accountant dublin". High intent, moderate competition.

**Seasonal triggers** — "self-assessment tax return ireland", "corporation tax deadline ireland", "payroll services dublin". Volume spikes around October–November (self-assessment) and year-end.

**Problem-based searches** — "missed tax deadline ireland", "revenue audit help ireland", "behind on accounts ireland". Lower volume, but very high intent — these people need help now.

**Business milestone searches** — "accountant for new business ireland", "company formation accountant dublin". These capture clients at the beginning of a long relationship.

## Budget: what actually makes sense

For a Dublin accounting firm, a realistic starting point is:

| Monthly Ad Spend | What to expect |
|---|---|
| < €600/month | Too thin — not enough data to optimise |
| €800–€1,500/month | Good foundation; 15–30 enquiries/month realistic |
| €1,500–€3,000/month | Strong market presence; useful for seasonal peaks |

The right budget also depends on your average client value. If a new bookkeeping client is worth €3,000/year and you retain them for three years, a €150 cost per lead becomes an obvious investment.

## The B2B buying cycle: what most campaigns miss

Most Google Ads agencies build accountancy campaigns as if they're selling impulse purchases. They aren't.

A business owner searching for an accountant in February might not make contact until April. They'll visit your site, read your about page, check your credentials, and ask around. **Remarketing is not optional — it's essential.**

A well-structured campaign for a Dublin accounting firm runs:

1. **Search campaigns** for high-intent service terms — drive the initial visit
2. **Remarketing** (Google Display or YouTube) — stay visible to people who visited but didn't convert
3. **Seasonal Search bursts** — increase budgets in October–November and January for tax deadline traffic

This structure is more complex than a single search campaign, but it reflects the reality of how accountancy clients actually buy.

## What a well-run campaign looks like

1. **Tight geographic targeting** — Dublin and surrounding counties; not "Ireland" unless you serve the whole country
2. **Phrase and exact match keywords only** — broad match wastes budget on irrelevant searches
3. **Strong negative keyword lists** — exclude "jobs", "courses", "software", "free", "DIY"
4. **Dedicated landing pages** — not your homepage; a page that speaks directly to the search
5. **Conversion tracking** — phone calls, contact form submissions, and quote requests tracked individually
6. **Monthly budget reviews** — accounting has strong seasonal patterns; your bids should reflect that

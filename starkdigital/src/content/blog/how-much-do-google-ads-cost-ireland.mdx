## The short answer

For a professional services firm in Dublin, expect to spend **€800–€2,000/month** in ad spend, plus a management fee of €400–€800/month if you work with an agency.

But the range is wide — so let's break it down properly.

## What drives the cost of Google Ads in Ireland?

There are two costs to consider:

1. **Ad spend** — the money that goes directly to Google
2. **Management fees** — what you pay an agency or specialist to run the campaigns

### Ad spend: what to expect by industry

Legal services (solicitors, barristers) are among the most competitive on Google in Ireland. Keywords like "conveyancing solicitor Dublin" or "employment law solicitor" can cost €8–€25 per click.

That sounds expensive — but if one client is worth €5,000–€50,000, the maths works very clearly in your favour.

For accountants and financial advisors, costs are lower — typically €3–€10 per click — but volumes are also smaller.

### A realistic starting budget

Most professional service businesses in Dublin need **at least €800–€1,000/month** in ad spend to gather enough data and generate meaningful results.

Below that threshold, you're often in a constant learning phase and won't see consistent returns.

## Management fees: what agencies charge in Ireland

Google Ads management fees in Ireland typically fall between **€400–€1,500/month** depending on:

- Number of campaigns and ad groups
- Whether conversion tracking needs to be set up from scratch
- Reporting requirements
- Ad spend levels (some agencies charge a percentage)
